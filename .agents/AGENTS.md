# ANTAI Project Rules

## Design & Layout Guidelines
- **NO BOXED CONTAINERS / NO CARDS WITH HEAVY BORDERS**: Never use boxed container cards (`.card`, `.glass-panel`, `.box`, heavy enclosed borders around content blocks).
- **FLUID & DYNAMIC LAYOUT**: Use open, fluid, floating typography, subtle glowing dividers, edge-to-edge layouts, and organic seamless elements instead of boxed containers.
- **COLOR PALETTE**: Imperial Crimson (`#ff003c`), Deep Obsidian Black (`#050508`), High-Contrast Pure White (`#ffffff`).
